Around Grand Josun Busan

Walks from the conference hotel: three for the daytime and one for the evening, plus a few places a short walk away. Choose one or more to see them on the map, then tap a numbered stop for details and links.

Google Maps has limited navigation in Korea. For walking and public transport directions, use Naver Map or KakaoMap; each stop links to all three.

Compiled November 2026; routes and opening hours will be re-checked before the conference. Walking times are estimates at an easy pace (4 km/h) and do not include stops. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Sources for each photo are listed under Photo credits.
